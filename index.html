About

I've been writing software professionally for over 15 years, mostly enterprise stuff during the day. Selinger Apps is my side project — a place to build things that are actually fun to make.

When I'm not coding, I play the clarinet, the trumpet or any instrument I get my fingers on in local bands, and spend time with my family in a small town near Freiburg.

Everything here is built with care. No dark patterns, no data harvesting, no subscription traps. Just useful tools that respect your time.
